02 / The agents

Spend a budget. Remember through the catalog. Refuse what you cannot prove.

  1. 01 Auditor Chooses where to look.sidq audit ranks every asset by the damage a lie about it would do, spends an explicit budget worst-first, and names what it did not reach. NOT VERIFIED is an answer, never a default. ↓
  2. 02 Memory Remembers through the catalog itself.sidq audit --resume reads the receipts previous runs wrote back: assets whose receipt still holds are skipped, the budget reaches assets no run has seen, and coverage converges — any Sidq instance resumes where any other stopped. No ledger to sync; the catalog is the ledger. ↓
  3. 03 Repair agent Refuses the plausible fix.sidq repair rejected the one-column PII fix — it resolved the finding and moved the leak downstream — and ships the 6-column closure the engine re-proved instead. Four of six checks get no proposal at all, each refusal with its reason recorded. —
