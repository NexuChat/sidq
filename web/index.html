03 The receipt

          
The next agent verifies for itself.

          
A receipt is not authority. A separate reader re-reads the graph context, re-checks policy hash and age, and answers on three axes it never collapses: does it apply, what did it decide, what may you do.


          

            
Current PASSContinue.

            
Current WARNReview or escalate.

            
Current BLOCKStop. Checked and refused — never shown as unverified.

            
NOT VERIFIEDMissing, stale, or unreadable. Check again.
